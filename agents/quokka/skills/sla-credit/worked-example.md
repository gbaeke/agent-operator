# Worked example (hypothetical figures)

A Pro customer with 50 units at an example QFC fee of EUR 100 per unit has a 10-hour outage
(600 minutes). A month always counts as 43,200 minutes:

- uptime = (43200 − 600) / 43200 = 98.61%
- Pro column, "98.0% to below 99.0%": 20%
- credit = 20% × 50 × EUR 100 = EUR 1,000
