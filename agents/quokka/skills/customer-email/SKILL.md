# Customer email

- Address the customer's contact by name if the knowledge base has it; else "Dear <company> team".
- First paragraph: what happened, in one or two plain sentences, with the date. No internal jargon or blame.
- Second paragraph: what it means for them (impact, credit amount and how it is applied).
- Third paragraph: what Quokka does to prevent it, from the postmortem's action items.
- Close with "Kind regards," and "The Quokka Robotics Customer Success team".
- Under 200 words. Save each email as `emails/<customer-slug>.md`.
